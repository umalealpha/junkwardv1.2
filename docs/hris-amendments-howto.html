For Unami, Dorothy & Thapelo · now live on omni

Dumelang,

You now have full HRIS editing access on omni. To keep the data clean and audited, every change is dual-approved — nothing touches the live record until a second person approves it. Here is how it works.

Making a change

  1. Open omni → HRIS → Amendments (the orange Amendments tile), or go to omni.alphadirect.co.bw/hris/amendments.
  2. Pick the employee from the list.
  3. Edit the field(s) you need — name, job title, department, phone, bank details, etc.
  4. Type a short reason (e.g. promotion, correction, bank update).
  5. Click Submit for approval. The record does not change yet.

omni then automatically emails the approver ("<your name> has made amendments, please approve") and emails you a confirmation of exactly what you submitted.

Approving a change

  1. On the same Amendments page, open Pending approvals.
  2. You will see the old value → new value for each field, plus the reason.
  3. Click Approve (applies it to the live record) or Reject.
  4. The person who requested it gets an email with the outcome.
Who approves what: Unami approves Dorothy's and Thapelo's changes. When Unami is the one making the change, it comes to me (CFO) to approve — so no one ever approves their own edit. That is the control; please don't try to work around it.
